Two of the underwater accounts bill their last month in August, which lifts blended margin further from September.

Cash fell to $10,736 from $15,221, even with $41,685 of shareholder contributions, because operating burn was $46,169. Contributions are now $466,173, approaching the $476,027 raise. Runway remains under a month, and raise timing continues to be the priority.
Three Accounts Roll Off After August
Three accounts billed their final month in August and drop off in September. Two of the three ran underwater, so removing them is margin-accretive, but the book loses about $3,350 of monthly revenue. Replacing it with margin-positive clients is the Q3 acquisition priority.
One Underwater Account Is Staying
One account that is not leaving ran underwater in August, billing $2,800 against $4,583 of dispatcher cost, a negative gross profit of about $1,783. Part of the August cost may reflect a mid-year dispatcher transition on the account, which could be transition or operating cost rather than recurring COGS, so reassess once the handoff settles before repricing or rescoping.
